About Us

Who we are

A registered, non-profit, non-denominational alliance committed to the spiritual, social and economic development of individuals and communities across the globe.

A vision born of divine calling

Rooted in faith since 1988

The International Ministerial Alliance of Churches (IMAC) stands as a testament to divine inspiration: a vision conceived in the heart of its founder during the first quarter of 1988.

With deep roots in the Christian faith, IMAC serves as a global conduit for empowering leaders to drive sustainable change within their local and international spheres of influence.
